FSAE Suspension Control Arms

May 2025 - January 2026

What?

  • Previous FSAE suspension control arms weighed 5 lbs with a factor of safety (FOS) of 8, presenting an opportunity for weight optimization.
  • Overall vehicle mass exceeded the team's targeted weight limit, requiring component-level weight reduction.
  • Minimizing control arm mass improves vehicle dynamics due to less unsprung mass.

How?

  • Developed redesigned suspension control arms using SolidWorks with a focus on optimizing tubing dimensions to minimize weight.
  • Performed load calculations to determine forces acting on the control arms under maximum braking, cornering, and acceleration conditions.
  • Utilized SolidWorks Simulation to conduct Finite Element Analysis (FEA) under maximum braking, cornering, and acceleration loading conditions, evaluating stress distributions and validating a calculated factor of safety of 3.0.
  • Evaluated 4130 chromoly steel tubing specifications to balance structural integrity, weight reduction, and manufacturability.

Results

  • Reduced control arm weight by 20%, from 5.0 lbs to 4.0 lbs.
  • Reduced excess structural safety margin by 61.25%, achieving a final factor of safety of 3.1 under simulated loading conditions.
  • Reduced the vehicle unsprung mass, supporting improved wheel responsiveness and vehicle dynamics.

FSAE Steering Rack Protection

April 2025

What?

  • Formula SAE car did not meet applicable SAE International safety regulations regarding steering system protection and risked disqualification.
  • Exposed steering rack and brake lines presented potential driver entanglement hazards.
  • Vehicle exceeded its design target weight, requiring a lightweight solution that minimized additional mass.

How?

  • Evaluated steering rack geometry, brake line routing, and surrounding chassis components to identify potential interference points.
  • Designed around existing mounting locations and packaging constraints to optimize geometry.
  • Developed a protective cover in SolidWorks to isolate steering and brake components from the driver.
  • Manufactured the protective piece from carbon steel sheet metal, utilizing tools such as angle grinders and sheet metal brakes.

Results

  • Designed a lightweight steering rack protection solution that ensured SAE regulatory compliance, contributing to the vehicle passing technical inspection for the first time in five years.
  • Eliminated potential driver exposure to moving steering components and brake lines.
  • Achieved a final component weight of 1.8 lbs, reducing additional vehicle mass by 50% compared to an alternative design requiring welded mounting points.

Climate Trend Analysis Model

March 2025

What?

  • Investigated historical climate trends by analyzing annual surface temperature changes and Pacific Ocean sea level measurements.
  • Explored unsupervised machine learning techniques to identify similarities, patterns, and distinct groupings within multidimensional climate datasets.
  • Sought to develop an interactive tool capable of visualizing climate data across multiple countries.

How?

  • Developed an interactive MATLAB App Designer application utilizing K-Means clustering to analyze and visualize historical climate data.
  • Implemented Z-score normalization to standardize variables and reduce scale-related bias in Euclidean distance calculations.
  • Integrated adjustable cluster configurations (K=1–6), centroid calculations, and interactive 2D/3D visualizations across four countries.
  • Evaluated clustering performance using average silhouette coefficients to determine the most effective cluster configuration.

Results

  • Identified K=2 as the optimal configuration among tested values, achieving an average silhouette coefficient of 0.4344, indicating moderate cluster separation.
  • Achieved a 34.7% higher silhouette coefficient compared with the next-best configuration (K=4).
  • Developed an interactive machine learning application enabling users to explore and compare historical climate observations through multidimensional clustering.
