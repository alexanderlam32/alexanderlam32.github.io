Engineering Projects

01 — Pump Rerate & Coupling Upgrade

Project Overview

Addressed pump cavitation caused by recent changes in discharge side piping while replacing obsolete couplings which have caused one pump to be placed out of service.

Technical Contributions & Results
  • Performed total dynamic head (TDH) calculations to develop a pump system resistance curve, conducted hydraulic calculations to determine an optimal impeller diameter, and completed torque calculations to select a replacement coupling.
  • Reduced pump cavitation and improved pump efficiency by 12%, generating a projected $88,000 in cost savings over 10 years.
  • Initiated, led, and closed a Management of Change (MOC) process for the proposed modifications, leading to field implementation.

03 — Hyper Compressor Cylinder Body Repair

Project Overview

Hyper compressor cylinder bodies would wear over time leaking Ethylene process to atmosphere, but were not repaired due to mechanical integrity concerns under high-pressure operating conditions creating the need for research into viable repair methods.

Technical Contributions & Results
  • Evaluated the technical and economic feasibility of multiple repair methods, comparing mechanical integrity considerations, repair costs, and long-term reliability against full component replacement.
  • Collaborated with equipment vendors and ExxonMobil Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to assess repair feasibility and technical limitations for considered hyper compressor repair options.
  • Presented the recommended repair strategy to unit business team, securing funding in the 2027 Annual Maintenance Plan (AMP) leading to $260,000 in cost avoidance compared with purchasing a new cylinder body.
